## How I work

I move from research and systems framing to interaction prototypes and production code. The goal is the same at each layer: make complex ideas legible, useful, and memorable.

Current tools include Figma, Paper, React, TypeScript, Three.js, Blender, and AI-assisted design and engineering workflows.
